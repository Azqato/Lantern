## Executive Summary

Lantern is a next-generation social platform centered around **culture rather than personal broadcasting**.

### Mission

> Help every person discover themselves and others through culture.

### Vision

Become the world's definitive cultural graph, organizing humanity's relationships with art, ideas, and creativity.

### Core Differentiator

Unlike traditional social networks that optimize for attention and virality, Lantern:
- Helps people discover who they are—and who they belong with
- Focuses on books, films, music, games, and other forms of art
- Combines structured media databases, AI-powered recommendations, and interest-based communities
- Creates a living map of human taste

## Problem Statement

### Current Social Platforms Gap

| Platform | Core Identity |
|----------|--------------|
| Instagram | Appearance |
| TikTok | Entertainment |
| LinkedIn | Career |
| X | Opinions |
| Facebook | Existing relationships |
| Reddit | Anonymous discussions |

**Missing:** None primarily organize around taste, intellectual identity, shared artistic interests, or long-term cultural journeys.

### User Pain Points

- Overwhelmed by content abundance
- Isolated in niche interests
- Frustrated by shallow recommendation systems
- Unable to discover people with truly similar cultural preferences
- No unified place to track and organize cultural consumption across media types

Cultural organization remains siloed:

| Medium | Platform |
|--------|----------|

| Movies | Letterboxd |
| Anime | MyAnimeList |
| Music | Spotify |

**Lantern's Opportunity:** Unify these fragmented ecosystems into a cohesive taste identity.

## Product Vision

### Taste Identity Layer

Lantern creates a persistent "Taste Identity Layer" for every user where every interaction builds a richer understanding of:

- What users love
- Why they love it
- How their tastes evolve
- Which people they naturally align with

### Discovery Model

Instead of following influencers, users discover:

- **Taste neighbors** — people with similar cultural preferences
- **Reading companions** — book enthusiasts in your circle
- **Film circles** — communities around cinema
- **Music tribes** — groups organized by musical taste
- **AI-generated cultural cohorts** — algorithmically identified communities

## Product Principles

### 1. Taste over Popularity
Follower count should not dominate discovery.

### 2. Depth over Virality
Encourage thoughtful reviews and discussions over viral moments.

### 3. Identity through Culture
A user's profile should answer: **"Who are you culturally?"**

### 4. AI as Guide, Not Replacement
AI assists discovery but does not replace human taste.

### 5. Global by Default
Support cross-cultural discovery:
- Chinese literature
- French cinema
- Japanese animation
- African music
- Latin American novels

## Core Features

### A. Cultural Profile

Each user maintains a dynamic profile including:

#### Collections
- Books
- Movies / TV
- Music
- Games
- Podcasts
- *Future media categories*

#### Status Tracking
- Completed
- Currently engaging
- Want to experience

#### Ratings
1-10 scale for all media

#### Favorites
Pinned cultural works displayed prominently

---

### B. Reviews

Support multiple formats:

- **Short reactions** — quick takes on media
- **Long essays** — detailed critical analysis
- **Quotes** — highlighted passages or moments
- **Photos** — visual documentation
- **Spoiler controls** — manage content spoilers

#### AI Assistance (Optional)
- Improve writing quality
- Generate summaries
- Extract themes
- Suggest organization

---

### C. AI Taste Graph

Continuously models user preferences and generates:

- **Hidden taste patterns** — deeper preference insights
- **Unexpected recommendations** — serendipitous discovery
- **Cultural archetypes** — user taste categorization
- **Similar users** — taste-based peer discovery

**Example Output:**
> "You enjoy magical realism, existential comedy, and emotionally restrained storytelling."

---

### D. Smart Cohorts

AI automatically forms dynamic micro-communities.

#### Characteristics
- Self-assembling based on shared taste
- No manual creation required
- Evolves over time as members' tastes evolve
- Enables organic community formation

#### Example
People who love:
- Murakami (author)
- Wong Kar-wai (filmmaker)
- Sufjan Stevens (musician)
- BoJack Horseman (show)

May naturally cluster together into a cohort.

---

### E. Discovery Feed

Curated feed combining:

- **Friends' activity** — what connections are consuming
- **Similar taste profiles** — recommendations from taste neighbors
- **AI recommendations** — personalized suggestions
- **Trending discussions** — popular reviews and conversations
- **Editorial collections** — curated lists by platform

**Ranking Priority:** Relevance and cultural compatibility over engagement metrics.

---

### F. Lists

User-created curated collections:

- Top 100 novels
- Best rainy day movies
- Beginner jazz albums
- Cyberpunk starter packs
- Thematic compilations

**Social Function:** Lists become shareable social objects for discovery and discussion.

---

### G. Search

Unified search across:

- **Titles** — exact media names
- **Creators** — authors, directors, musicians, developers
- **Genres** — broad categorization
- **Themes** — deeper conceptual search
- **Users** — find taste neighbors
- **Lists** — discover curated collections
- **Cohorts** — find communities

#### Future: Natural Language Search
**Example:** "Books that feel like autumn in New England."

## User Journey

### New User Onboarding

1. **Sign up** — Create account with email or social auth
2. **Import media history** (optional) — Connect Goodreads, Letterboxd, Spotify, etc.
3. **Select favorite works** — Quickly identify 10-20 beloved pieces
4. **AI generates initial taste profile** — System creates baseline understanding
5. **Join suggested cohorts** — Get invited to algorithmically matched communities
6. **Discovery feed populates** — Begin seeing personalized recommendations

### Returning User Engagement Loop

- Log media consumed
- Write reviews
- Discover recommendations
- Engage with cohort discussions
- Refine cultural identity
- Create and share lists
- Connect with taste neighbors

## AI Strategy

### Layer 1: Recommendation Engine
Suggests media based on user history and preferences.

### Layer 2: Taste Embedding Model
Every user and every work exists in a shared vector space, enabling taste-based similarity calculations.

### Layer 3: Community Generation
Smart Cohorts algorithm automatically identifies natural groupings of users with compatible tastes.

### Layer 4: Cultural Copilot (Future)
Advanced AI that answers complex cultural questions.

**Example Query:**
> "What book should I read after finishing Anna Karenina if I want something emotionally similar but set in modern Asia?"

## Business Model

### Phase 1: Network Growth
**Free product** with **affiliate link auto-embedding** (Prime Video, Audible, etc.)

Focus on network effects and user acquisition.

### Phase 2: Creator Tools
Premium features for creators and curators:

- **CMS** — Catalog tracking (private or public)
- **Creator Profile** — Enhanced branding and analytics
- **Premium newsletters** — Cultural commentary distribution
- **Community management** — Tools for cohort leaders
- **Analytics** — Audience insights

### Phase 3: Enterprise & Advertising
- **Advertising** — Targeted campaigns based on cultural data
- **Publisher/Studio Insights** — Anonymized audience intelligence
- **Data licensing** — Aggregated trend data
