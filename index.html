What Lantern does

01

Your taste identity.

Every book read, film finished, album loved — logged, rated, and reflected back to you as a living cultural profile. An AI-generated Cultural Archetype. Five Taste Dimensions. A portrait of who you actually are.

02

Your people.

Not who you follow — who you belong with. Lantern finds Taste Neighbors: people whose cultural graph overlaps with yours in ways that matter. Connected by what you love, not by who you happen to know.

03

Your community.

AI-generated Smart Cohorts form automatically around shared taste — no admins, no group creation, no manual joining. Just people who inevitably love the same things, gathered in the same place.

The principles

Taste over Popularity

Follower counts don't determine what you discover. Cultural compatibility does. The best-rated work by someone with 12 followers matters more than a viral take from someone with 2 million.

Depth over Virality

Thoughtful reviews over hot takes. Long books over short clips. Lantern is built for people who sit with things — who watch a film twice, who underline passages, who return to albums years later.

Global by Default

Bong Joon-ho. Toni Morrison. Edward Yang. Hirokazu Kore-eda. Solange. Great culture crosses every border. Lantern is built for all of it — not as a category, but as the default.
